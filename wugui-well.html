烏鬼井The Wugui Well

台南一條安靜的巷子裡，藏著台灣最古老的水井之一。它也有一個令人感傷的名字。古文獻記載，「烏鬼」指的是深膚色的勞工。他們是荷蘭東印度公司從東南亞帶來的。將近四百年前，他們挖了這口井。它鑿於 1653 年。井水豐沛、終年不涸。直到今天，井底仍然有水。好幾代人的時間裡，它供應附近港口往來船隻的飲水。當年的船隻，都指望著這口井。這個名字沒有經得起時代的檢驗；但他們的工程經得起。如今它是指定古蹟：住宅巷弄裡，一圈小小的、樸素的石井欄。古井在自強街的巷弄裡，舊名「大銃街」：小北門上的小砲，被誤以為是大砲！這一帶是舊城的軍事重地「鎮北坊」；如今巷子裡藏著百年餅舖和開基天后宮。

Down a quiet lane in Tainan hides one of the oldest wells in Taiwan. It also has one of the saddest names. Old records say Wugui referred to dark-skinned laborers. They were brought here from Southeast Asia by the Dutch East India Company. They dug this well nearly four hundred years ago. It is dated to 1653. Its water was abundant and never ran dry. Even today, there is water at the bottom. For generations, it supplied the ships calling at the nearby ports. Ships once depended on this water. The name has aged poorly; their work has not. Today the well is a designated monument: a small, humble ring of stone in a residential lane. The lane is on Ziqiang Street, once called Great Cannon Street. A small cannon on the Little North Gate was mistaken for a big one! This was the military quarter of the old city, the Zhenbei Ward. Today its lanes hide old pastry shops and the Kaiji Tianhou Temple.

Why it matters & history

烏鬼井是台灣最古老的水井之一。將近四百年前，荷蘭東印度公司從東南亞帶來的勞工挖了這口井，井水終年不涸，長年供應港口船隻；它提醒我們記得那些沒有留下名字的人。

The Wugui Well is one of the oldest wells in Taiwan. Nearly four hundred years ago, laborers brought from Southeast Asia by the Dutch East India Company dug it, and for generations it supplied ships in the nearby harbor.

  1. 1653井鑿成。The well is dug.
  2. 清代 Qing era文獻記載挖井的是被稱為「烏鬼」的深膚色勞工。Records describe the diggers as dark-skinned laborers.
  3. 今天 Today住宅巷弄裡一圈樸素的石井欄，為指定古蹟，井底仍有水。A plain stone ring in a quiet lane, now a listed monument, with water still at the bottom.

認識景點

About the place

水井：挖到地下取水的深洞。烏鬼井是一圈小小的石井欄。 勞工：做粗重體力工作的人。這口井就是勞工挖的。 乾涸：水用完、乾掉。相傳這口井從未乾涸。 指定古蹟：政府正式指定保護的古蹟。烏鬼井現在就是。

well: A deep hole dug in the ground to get water. This one is a small ring of stone. laborer: A worker who does hard physical work. Laborers dug this well. run dry: To have no water left. This well is said never to have run dry. designated monument: A place the government officially protects. The well is one today.

無名的：沒有名字的。挖井的勞工沒有留下名字。 荷蘭東印度公司：荷蘭的貿易公司，把勞工從東南亞帶到這裡。 東南亞：中國以南的區域。勞工來自東印度公司在那裡的屬地。 文獻、紀錄：古代留下的文字資料。清代文獻記載了挖井的人。

unnamed: Without a name. The workers who dug the well left no names. the Dutch East India Company: A Dutch trading company. It brought the laborers here from Southeast Asia. Southeast Asia: The region south of China. The laborers came from the company's lands there. records: Old written documents. Qing records tell us who dug the well.

港口：船停靠的地方。古井供應附近港口船隻的用水。 住宅巷弄：住家旁的小巷子。古井就藏在裡面。 大銃街：自強街的舊名。一門小砲被誤認成大砲。 軍事重地：軍隊駐紮的地區。鎮北坊是舊城的軍事重地。

port: A place where ships stop. The well supplied ships calling at nearby ports. residential lane: A small street where people live. The well hides in one. Great Cannon Street: The old name of Ziqiang Street. A small cannon was mistaken for a big one. military quarter: An area for soldiers. The Zhenbei Ward was the old city's military quarter.

餅舖：賣糕餅的店。這一帶藏著百年餅舖。

pastry shop: A shop that sells cakes and pastries. Century-old ones hide in these lanes.
