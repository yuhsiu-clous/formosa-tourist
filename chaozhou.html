台灣府三山國王廟The Sanshan Guowang Temple

這座廟會讓你停下腳步，因為它長得完全不一樣。周圍的廟大多是閩南風格：燕尾脊、濃烈的色彩、繁複的雕飾。這座是潮州風格：線條收斂、黑白為主、安靜典雅，是全台僅存保存完整的潮州式廟宇。「三山國王」是廣東潮州三座名山的山神。祂們是潮州與客家移民走到哪裡都奉祀的守護神。清代旅居府城的潮州人創建了它，兼作廟宇與會館，是異鄉人的家。連匠師和建材都來自潮州原鄉。讀讀屋頂：灰色灰梗仰瓦、翠綠瓦當、紅色封簷板，這種配色全台南僅此一家。三廟一字排開：三山國王居中，左邊是天后聖母祠，右邊是韓文公祠。在韓文公祠抬頭看樑上的螃蟹：兩隻螃蟹、兩個甲，就是金榜祝福。

This temple will stop you in your tracks, because it looks completely different. Most temples around it are Minnan style, with swallowtail ridges, blazing colors and crowded carvings. This one is Chaozhou style: restrained lines, a black-and-white palette, and quiet elegance. It is the only fully intact Chaozhou-style temple left in Taiwan. Sanshan Guowang means Kings of the Three Mountains. They are the guardian deities of three mountains in Chaozhou, Guangdong. They are the patron gods of Chaozhou and Hakka migrants everywhere. Chaozhou natives living in the city founded it in the Qing era. It was both their temple and their guild hall, a home away from home. The craftsmen and materials came from Chaozhou itself. Read the roof: gray ash-ridge tiling, jade-green eave tiles, and a red fascia board. You will see this palette nowhere else in Tainan. Three temples stand in a row: the Kings in the center, Mazu on the left, and Han Yu on the right. In the Han Yu shrine, look up at the crabs on the beam. Two crabs mean two jia, a blessing for exam success.

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history

台灣府三山國王廟是全台僅存保存完整的潮州式廟宇。周圍多是色彩濃烈的閩南廟，這座卻線條收斂、黑白素雅，連匠師和建材都來自潮州，記錄了廣東移民在府城的足跡。

The Sanshan Guowang Temple is the only fully preserved Chaozhou-style temple in Taiwan. Its quiet lines and black-and-white tones stand apart from the bright southern Fujian temples around it, and even its craftsmen and materials came from Chaozhou.

  1. 清代 Qing era旅居府城的潮州人創建，兼作廟宇與同鄉會館。Chaozhou people living in the city build it as both a temple and a guild hall.
  2. 乾隆年間 Qianlong era乾隆皇帝賜「褒忠」匾。The Qianlong Emperor grants a plaque praising loyalty.
  3. 今天 Today三廟並列：三山國王居中，左為天后聖母祠，右為韓文公祠；國定古蹟。Three shrines stand side by side, with the Three Mountain Kings in the middle. A national monument.

認識景點

About the place

守護神：三山國王是來自潮州的守護神。 移民：潮州與客家移民走到哪裡都拜三山國王。 旅居者：暫時住在異鄉的人。 會館：同鄉人聚會、寄宿的地方，是異鄉人的家。

guardian deity: The Kings of the Three Mountains are guardian deities from Chaozhou. migrant: Chaozhou and Hakka migrants pray to the Three Kings everywhere they go. sojourner: A sojourner lives in a place for a while, far from home. guild hall: A meeting place and home away from home for people from the same hometown.

收斂的、內斂的：安靜、不張揚；這座廟的線條很收斂。 木雕：潮州木雕：樑上有螃蟹、小魚、水草和蛤蜊。 封簷板：屋簷邊的板子；這裡是紅色的，上面是翠綠瓦當。 嵌瓷：潮州的剪黏工藝，叫做嵌瓷。

restrained: Quiet and controlled, not showy. The temple's lines are restrained. woodcarving: Chaozhou woodcarving shows crabs, a small fish, waterweed and clams on a beam. fascia board: The board along the roof edge. Here it is red, under jade-green eave tiles. qianci: Inlaid porcelain: the Chaozhou name and manner of the cut-porcelain craft.

文官、士大夫：韓愈是曾被貶到潮州的唐代大文豪。 花瓶形門洞：「瓶」諧音「平」，門洞祝你平安。 諧音：蟹殼的「甲」，諧音科舉的「甲」。 匾額：「褒忠」匾是乾隆皇帝所賜。

scholar-official: Han Yu was a great Tang scholar-official once exiled to Chaozhou. vase-shaped doorway: Vase puns on peace, so the doorways wish you peace. homophone: Crab shells, jia, sound like the top ranks of the imperial exam. plaque: The Bao Zhong plaque, honoring the loyal, was given by the Qianlong Emperor.

國定古蹟：它是國定古蹟，熬過艱難歲月依然完整。

national monument: The temple is a national monument, still intact after hard times.
