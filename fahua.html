福爾摩沙小導遊臺南 Tainan・中西區

法華寺Fahua Temple

認識隱士李茂春的夢蝶園，怎麼在三百多年裡長成府城古剎，還有寺裡值得細看的碑、橋與壁畫。

Learn how a hermit's butterfly garden grew into one of Tainan's great old temples.

在成為寺廟之前，府城的這個角落，是一個人的夢。1664 年，文人李茂春隨明鄭渡海來臺。他不肯做官，在這裡搭起茅廬。好友陳永華借莊周夢蝶的典故，為它取名「夢蝶園」。1675 年李茂春過世，僧友把茅齋改成小庵；1684 年擴建為佛寺，改名法華寺。清代增建火神廟，又鑿出半月形的南湖划龍舟，文人雅士在湖畔聚會。二戰炸彈毀去大半殿宇，戰後依原格局重建。來找找夢蝶園記碑、延壽橋和水墨壁畫。

Before it was a temple, this quiet corner of old Tainan was one man's dream. In 1664, the scholar Li Mao-chun crossed to Taiwan with the Zheng court. He refused office and built a thatched hut here. His friend Chen Yong-hua named it the Dream Butterfly Garden, after Zhuangzi's famous dream of becoming a butterfly. After Li died in 1675, monks made his hut a shrine. In 1684, it grew into a Buddhist temple named Fahua. The Qing added a fire god hall and a half-moon lake for dragon-boat races. Poets and scholars gathered on its banks. Wartime bombs destroyed much of it, but it was rebuilt on the old layout. Look for the garden stele, the Longevity Bridge and the ink murals.

認識景點

About the place

作夢的人The dreamer

隨鄭經渡海：李茂春是明鄭文人，1664 年隨鄭經的大撤退渡海來臺。 溪畔茅廬：他不肯做官，在溪畔搭茅廬，植蔬種竹。 李菩薩：他終日誦經，人稱「李菩薩」。 摯友陳永華：陳永華是明鄭的諮議參軍，也是李茂春的好朋友。

Crossing with the Zheng court: Li Mao-chun, a Ming loyalist scholar, crossed to Taiwan in 1664. A thatched hut: He refused office and built a hut to grow vegetables and bamboo. Li the Bodhisattva: He chanted sutras all day, so people called him Li the Bodhisattva. His friend Chen Yong-hua: His close friend was Chen Yong-hua, a top minister of the Zheng court.

夢蝶園：陳永華借莊周夢蝶的典故為園子命名，還親筆寫下《夢蝶園記》。

The Dream Butterfly Garden: Chen named it after Zhuangzi's butterfly dream and wrote the garden's record himself.

從小庵到古剎From shrine to great temple

準提庵：1675 年李茂春去世，僧友把茅齋改鋪陶瓦，成了小小的準提庵。 改名法華寺：1684 年臺灣知府蔣毓英擴建為佛寺，改名法華寺。 火神廟：1708 年增建火神廟；後來改建在寺的右側。 康熙銅鐘：1713 年住持募款鑄造的銅鐘，至今仍在。

A small shrine: After Li died in 1675, his monk friends re-roofed the hut and made it a shrine. Renamed Fahua: In 1684, prefect Chiang Yu-ying rebuilt it as Fahua Temple. The fire god hall: A fire god hall was added in 1708, later rebuilt on the right side. The bronze bell: A bronze bell cast in 1713 still survives.

南湖競渡：1765 年知府蔣允焄在寺前鑿半月形南湖供端午競渡，湖畔建了半月樓與南湖書院。

South Lake: In 1765, prefect Chiang Yun-hsun dug a half-moon lake for dragon-boat races, with a pavilion and an academy.

詩人、戰火與重建Poets, war and rebuilding

1914 年大整修：捐助者裡有三郊的許藏春，就是神農街那位郊商。 詩社聚會：1919 年，詩社「南社」移到寺裡聚會。 戰火與重建：二戰時殿宇大半被毀；戰後依原有格局重建。 市定古蹟：1985 年列為古蹟，今為臺南市定古蹟。

The 1914 restoration: Donors included Xu Cang-chun, a merchant of the Three Guilds. Poets' societies: From 1919, the Nan Society of poets met here. War and rebuilding: Bombs destroyed much of it, and it was rebuilt on the old layout. A listed monument: Since 1985 it has been a listed historic monument.

法華夢蝶：「法華夢蝶」是 1956 年選出的臺南十二勝景之一。

A classic scene: "Fahua's Butterfly Dream" is one of Tainan's twelve classic scenes.
