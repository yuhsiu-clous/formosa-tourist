散步補給站｜北區百年老味Refuel Stops: Century-Old Flavors of the North

補給時間到！來嚐嚐北區的百年老味。舊來發餅舖 1875 年開爐，就在開基天后宮旁的總爺古街上。招牌是中空的椪餅，拜拜與做月子都少不了它。椪餅打一顆蛋、加麻油煮，是台南人的月子點心。連得堂是日治時期創業的手工煎餅老舖，味噌、雞蛋煎餅一片片手工壓製。每人限購兩包；在老街上排隊，本身就是行程。阿憨鹹粥：飯粒泡在魚湯裡，鋪上現燙魚肚，配一根油條，天亮就要來。鴨母寮市場 1918 年開設，是百年早市：空著肚子來，一攤一攤吃過去。想吃一桌台菜：東香台菜海味料理是必比登推介，由掌廚三十年的總舖師領軍。

Time to refuel! Let's taste the century-old flavors of the North District. Jiu-lai-fa Pastry Shop opened in 1875, beside the Kaiji Mazu Temple on the old Zongye Street. Its star is the hollow peng-bing, the puffed cake used in temple offerings and postpartum tonics. Cooked with an egg and sesame oil, peng-bing is Tainan's postpartum treat. Lian-de-tang is a Japanese-era family bakery. Its miso and egg crackers are pressed by hand. They are so popular that each visitor may buy only two packs. The queue on the old street is part of the experience. At A-han Savory Congee, rice grains swim in fish broth, topped with fresh milkfish belly. Eat it with a fried cruller at sunrise. Yamuliao Market opened in 1918. It is a century-old morning market. Come hungry and graze the stalls. For a banquet table, Dong-xiang Taiwanese Cuisine is a Bib Gourmand kitchen led by a chef with thirty years as a banquet master.

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history

北區的老街和市場藏著好幾家百年老店：開基天后宮旁的老餅舖、手工煎餅、清晨的鹹粥，還有百年早市鴨母寮市場。散步走累了，就在這裡補給。

North District's old streets and markets hide shops that are a century old: a cake shop beside the Little Mazu Temple, hand-pressed crackers, early-morning fish congee and the Yamuliao morning market.

  1. 1875舊來發餅舖在總爺古街開爐，招牌是中空的椪餅。Jiu Lai Fa bakery opens on Zongye Street, famous for its hollow pastry.
  2. 1918鴨母寮市場開設，至今是熱鬧的早市。Yamuliao Market opens and is still a busy morning market.
  3. 日治時期 Japanese era連得堂手工煎餅創業。Lian De Tang starts making hand-pressed crackers.

認識景點

About the place

椪餅：中空的「膨」餅；林百貨頂樓的椪餅椅，本尊就是它。 拜拜的供品：在廟裡拜神的食物；椪餅是拜拜少不了的。 月子補品：幫助產婦補身的食物；椪餅打蛋加麻油煮。 麻油：用芝麻做成、很香的油。

peng-bing: A hollow puffed cake. You may have sat on a peng-bing chair at Hayashi's rooftop! temple offering: Food offered to the gods at a temple. Peng-bing is used in temple offerings. postpartum tonic: Food that helps a new mother get strong. Peng-bing is cooked with an egg and sesame oil. sesame oil: A dark, fragrant oil made from sesame seeds.

煎餅：又薄又脆的餅；連得堂有味噌和雞蛋煎餅。 手工壓製的：用手一片一片壓出來的。 每人限購兩包：每人只能買兩包，所以排隊也是行程的一部分。 粥、鹹粥：用很多湯水煮的米；台南的鹹粥是飯粒泡在魚湯裡。

crackers: Thin, crisp baked biscuits. Lian-de-tang makes miso and egg crackers. hand-pressed: Pressed by hand, one piece at a time. only two packs each: Each visitor may buy only two packs, so the queue is part of the fun. congee: Rice cooked in lots of liquid. In Tainan, rice grains swim in fish broth.

虱目魚肚：虱目魚最肥嫩的魚肚；虱目魚是府城的靈魂之魚。 油條：長條形的炸麵，天亮時配粥吃。 一攤一攤吃過去：每一攤吃一點；鴨母寮市場就要這樣逛。 總舖師：掌廚辦桌的大廚；東香的總舖師掌廚三十年。

milkfish belly: The rich, soft belly of the milkfish, Tainan's soul fish. fried cruller: A long stick of fried dough, eaten with congee at sunrise. graze the stalls: Try a little food at one stall after another. Do it at Yamuliao Market! banquet master: A chef who cooks the big ban-doh feasts. Dong-xiang's chef has thirty years as one.
