新化老街Xinhua Old Street

歡迎來到新化老街。它的舊名「大目降」是西拉雅語，意思是山林之地。1920 年代，商人把街屋改建成巴洛克風格，有柱子、花草和家徽。老街上的麻油行、餅舖和老市場，很多店家已經傳了好幾代。慢慢走，抬頭看，每一面立面都是一張百年前的名片。

Welcome to Xinhua Old Street. Its old name, Tavocan, is a Siraya word for a place of mountain forests. In the 1920s, merchants rebuilt their shophouses in baroque style, with columns, garlands and family crests. Along the street, sesame oil makers, bakeries and the old market have been run by the same families for generations. Walk slowly and look up. Every facade is a business card from a hundred years ago.

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history

新化老街是全台保存最完整的巴洛克街景之一。新化舊名「大目降」，來自西拉雅語，意思是山林之地；1920 年代，商人把街屋改建成有柱子、花草和家徽的華麗立面，很多老店傳承至今。

Xinhua Old Street has one of Taiwan's best-kept rows of Baroque-style shop fronts. Its old name, Tavocan, comes from the Siraya language and means a place of mountain forests. In the 1920s, merchants rebuilt their shop houses with columns, flowers and family crests, and many old shops are still run by the same families.

  1. 清代以前 Before the Qing西拉雅族在這裡生活，地名「大目降」由此而來。The Siraya people live here, and the place name Tavocan comes from their language.
  2. 1920 年代 1920s新化商人把街屋改建成巴洛克風格立面。Xinhua merchants rebuild their shop houses with Baroque fronts.

認識景點

About the place

大目降：新化舊名「大目降」，西拉雅語的意思是山林之地。 巴洛克：新化老街是全台保存最完整的巴洛克街景之一。 立面：抬頭看，每一面立面都在跟鄰居較勁。 街屋：兩層樓的街屋在一九二〇、三〇年代改建。

Tavocan: Xinhua's old name, Tavocan, is a Siraya word for a place of mountain forests. baroque: Xinhua has one of the best-preserved baroque streetscapes in Taiwan. facade: Look up: every facade competes with its neighbors. shophouse: The two-story shophouses were rebuilt in the 1920s and 30s.

家徽、山頭徽飾：每個山頭徽飾都不一樣，裡面有家號或行業。 壁柱：是貼在建築正面的扁平柱子。 山牆：是立面頂端裝飾的部分。 花草浮雕：牆上裝飾著花草浮雕。

crest: Every crest is different, with the owner's name or trade inside. pilaster: Pilasters are flat columns on the front of the building. pediment: The pediment is the decorated top of the facade. floral relief: Floral reliefs decorate the walls.

洗石子：家徽是用洗石子做成的。 麻油：這裡的麻油行已經傳了好幾代。 餅：老家族仍在同樣的立面後面賣餅。 公有市場：舊公有市場重生為美食廣場。

washed terrazzo: Family crests were made in washed terrazzo. sesame oil: A sesame-oil shop has run here for generations. biscuit: Old families still sell biscuits behind the same facades. public market: The old public market has been reborn as a food hall.
