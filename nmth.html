國立臺灣歷史博物館National Museum of Taiwan History

歡迎來到位在安南區的國立臺灣歷史博物館。博物館就蓋在昔日台江內海的海底上；荷蘭人駛入的年代，這裡還是一片汪洋。一面太陽能光電雲牆立在水池邊，就像昔日潟湖上的風帆。館內，水牛車涉水走向錨泊的帆船，因為港口太淺，大船進不來。你可以登上移民船，走過整條街屋立面。這裡的歷史不在玻璃櫃後面，而在你身邊。

Welcome to the National Museum of Taiwan History, in Annan District. The museum stands on the bottom of the old Taijiang Inland Sea. This land was open water when the Dutch sailed in. Its wall of solar panels rises beside a lake, like sails on the old lagoon. Inside, water buffalo carts wade through the surf to anchored junks, because the harbor was too shallow for ships. You can board an immigration boat and walk down a street of shophouses. History here is not behind glass. It is around you. Let's explore!

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history

國立臺灣歷史博物館講述整座島從先民到今天的故事。博物館就蓋在昔日台江內海淤積出的土地上；館內可以登上移民船、走過整條街屋，歷史就在身邊。

The National Museum of Taiwan History tells the story of the whole island, from its first peoples to today. It stands on land that was once the Taijiang Inland Sea, and inside you can board a migrant ship and walk down a full street of old shop fronts.

  1. 1624荷蘭人進駐大員。The Dutch arrive at Tayouan.
  2. 1661–1662鄭成功登陸，荷蘭人投降離台。Koxinga lands, and the Dutch surrender and leave.
  3. 1683–1684清軍入台，翌年設台灣府。Qing forces arrive and set up Taiwan Prefecture.
  4. 1823台江內海開始淤積，日後成為博物館所在的土地。The inland sea begins to silt up, creating the land the museum stands on.
  5. 1895台灣割讓給日本。Taiwan is ceded to Japan.

認識景點

About the place

歷史博物館：博物館講述整座島的故事，從先民一直到今天。 太陽能板：一千三百多片太陽能板，立在內海曾經閃耀的地方。 新生地：博物館坐落在台江內海淤積而成的新生地上。 實景模型：一比一的實景模型，讓你走進清代的街道。

history museum: The museum tells the whole island's story, from the first peoples to today. solar panel: About 1,350 solar panels stand where the inland sea once shone. reclaimed land: The museum stands on land reclaimed from the Taijiang Inland Sea. diorama: Life-sized dioramas let you walk into a Qing-era street.

牛車：涉水走向帆船，把貨物運上岸。 戎克船、帆船：大型帆船停在外海，因為港口太淺。 移民：登上移民船，想像橫渡海峽的旅程。 街屋：在館內走過一整條街屋立面。

ox cart: Ox carts waded out to the junks to carry goods ashore. junk: Big junks anchored offshore because the harbor was too shallow. immigration: Board the immigration boat and imagine crossing the strait. shophouse: Walk past a street of shophouse facades inside the museum.

熱蘭遮城：1624 年，荷蘭人進駐大員，日後築成熱蘭遮城。 普羅民遮城：1653 年，荷蘭人建普羅民遮城，即今赤崁樓前身。 鄭成功：1661 年鄭成功登陸，荷蘭人在 1662 年初投降。 孔廟：1665 年，鄭經創建孔廟並設立學校。

Fort Zeelandia: In 1624, the Dutch set up at Tayouan, later building Fort Zeelandia. Fort Provintia: In 1653, the Dutch built Fort Provintia, today's Chihkan Tower. Koxinga: In 1661, Koxinga landed, and the Dutch surrendered in early 1662. Confucius Temple: In 1665, Zheng Jing founded the Confucius Temple and its school.

台灣府：清軍在 1683 年入台，翌年設立台灣府。 淤積：1823 年曾文溪改道，台江內海開始淤積。 日治時期：1895 年，台灣割讓給日本。 米：平原的稻米從台南的港口運出。

Taiwan Prefecture: The Qing took Taiwan in 1683 and set up Taiwan Prefecture the next year. silt: In 1823, the Zengwen River shifted and the inland sea began to silt up. Japanese era: In 1895, Taiwan was ceded to Japan. rice: Rice from the plains was shipped from Tainan's harbors.

鹽：海邊曬的鹽是大宗出口貨之一。 樟腦：山區的樟腦是珍貴的出口貨。 糖：、米、鹽、樟腦是老台南的大宗出口貨。

salt: Salt from the coast was one of the great exports. camphor: Camphor from the mountains was a precious export. sugar: Sugar, rice, salt and camphor were old Tainan's great exports.
