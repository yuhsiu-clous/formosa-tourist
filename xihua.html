西華堂Xihua Hall

歡迎來到西華堂，台南保存最好的齋堂之一。它創建於 1750 年（乾隆十五年），由四位渡海來台的居士大德建基。西華堂屬於齋教的「金幢派」。信眾在家修行、持齋茹素，但不剃度出家。還記得德化堂嗎？齋堂是嚴苛年代裡，女性的一方安靜庇護所。看看平直的屋脊和素雅的白牆。它看起來更像書香門第，而不是廟宇。大殿主祀三寶佛，兩側奉觀世音與地藏王菩薩。最深處的房間稱為「內家鄉」，是全堂最神聖的空間，供奉金幢教祖。如今這裡是比丘尼的道場，也是市定古蹟。請安靜參觀。

Welcome to Xihua Hall, one of the best-preserved vegetarian halls in Tainan. It was founded in 1750 by four lay masters from the mainland. It belongs to the Jinchuang school of the Zhaijiao tradition. Its members were lay Buddhists. They kept vegetarian discipline, but they did not become ordained monks or nuns. Remember Dehua Hall? A vegetarian hall was a quiet shelter for women in a harsh age. Look at the straight ridgelines and the gentle whitewashed walls. It feels more like a scholar's residence than a temple. The main hall honors the Three Treasure Buddhas, flanked by Guanyin and Ksitigarbha. The innermost room, the Inner Homeland, is the most sacred space. It is reserved for the founding patriarchs. Today it is a nunnery and a city-designated monument. Please visit quietly.

Why it matters & history

西華堂是台南保存最好的齋堂之一。齋教信眾在家修行、持齋茹素而不出家；在過去，齋堂也是女性安身的庇護所。白牆平脊、像書香門第的建築，是認識台灣民間佛教的好地方。

Xihua Hall is one of the best-kept vegetarian halls in Tainan. Its members practiced Buddhism at home and kept a meat-free diet without becoming monks or nuns, and such halls once gave women a quiet refuge.

  1. 1750四位渡海來台的居士創建，屬齋教金幢派。Four lay Buddhists from across the strait found the hall, part of the Jintong branch of Zhai Buddhism.
  2. 清代至今 Qing to today主殿奉三寶佛，最深處的「內家鄉」供奉金幢教祖。The main hall honors the Three Buddhas; the innermost room honors the founder of the Jintong branch.
  3. 今天 Today比丘尼的道場，市定古蹟，請安靜參觀。A home for Buddhist nuns and a city monument. Please visit quietly.

認識景點

About the place

齋堂：創建於 1750 年的西華堂，是台南保存最好的齋堂之一。 在家修行的佛教徒：不出家、在家修行的佛教徒。 受戒出家的：正式受戒出家。齋教信眾不剃度出家。 素食的、吃素的人：不吃肉。信眾持齋茹素。

vegetarian hall: Xihua Hall, founded in 1750, is one of the best-preserved vegetarian halls in Tainan. lay Buddhist: A Buddhist who worships without becoming a monk or nun. ordained: Formally made a monk or nun. Zhaijiao members did not become ordained. vegetarian: Eating no meat. Members kept vegetarian discipline.

金幢派：西華堂屬於齋教金幢派。 粉刷成白色的：刷成白色的。西華堂有素雅的白牆。 平直的屋脊：屋頂最上面的那條線；這裡是平直的。 座向：建築面對的方向。西華堂保留原有的座向。

the Jinchuang school: Xihua Hall belongs to the Jinchuang school of the Zhaijiao tradition. whitewashed: Painted white. The hall has gentle whitewashed walls. straight ridgeline: The top line of the roof. Here it is straight, not curved. orientation: The direction a building faces. The hall keeps its original orientation.

書香門第：它看起來更像書香門第，而不是廟宇。 三寶佛：大殿主祀三寶佛。 觀世音與地藏王菩薩：祂們奉在三寶佛的兩側。 內家鄉：最深處的房間，供奉金幢教祖。

scholar's residence: It feels more like a scholar's residence than a temple. Three Treasure Buddhas: The main hall honors the Three Treasure Buddhas. Guanyin and Ksitigarbha: They stand on the two sides of the Three Treasure Buddhas. Inner Homeland: The innermost room, reserved for the school's founding patriarchs.

比丘尼的道場：比丘尼修行的地方。如今西華堂是比丘尼的道場。 市定古蹟：由市政府指定保護的古蹟。請安靜參觀。

nunnery: A home for Buddhist nuns. Today the hall serves as a nunnery. city-designated monument: A historic site protected by the city. Please visit quietly.
