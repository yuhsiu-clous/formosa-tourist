五妃廟Temple of the Five Concubines

認識寧靖王與五妃的故事、全臺罕見的廟墓合一建築，以及三百年來守護她們的人。

Learn the story of the prince and the five ladies, a rare temple-tomb, and the people who cared for it.

安靜的綠地公園裡，有一座小廟：廟殿和後方的墳塚合為一體。1683 年清軍水師擊敗鄭軍。明朝的寧靖王決定不投降。五位妃子選擇追隨他。王爺把她們葬在南門外的荒丘，墳前無碑。六十多年後的 1746 年，兩位巡臺御史被故事打動，修墓建廟。神龕正後方就是墳塚本體；廟旁小祠祭祀兩位忠心的侍宦。1927 年婦女組織出資整修；今天它是國定古蹟，也是全臺第一個古蹟保存區。

In a quiet green park stands a small temple where the hall and the tomb behind it are one. In 1683, the Qing fleet defeated the Zheng navy. The Prince of Ningjing, a Ming prince, decided he would not surrender. His five concubines chose to follow him. He buried them on a lonely hill outside the south gate, with no marker. More than sixty years later, in 1746, two Qing censors were moved by the story and built a proper tomb and shrine. Behind the altar rises the grave mound itself. Beside the temple, a small shrine honors two loyal attendants. In 1927, a women's association restored the temple. Today it is a national monument in Taiwan's first heritage conservation area.

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history
  1. 王爺與五妃1683 年，明鄭走到了最後；一位親王和五位女子，做了同一個決定。In 1683, the Zheng kingdom reached its end, and a prince and five ladies made one choice.
  2. 廟墓合一繞到殿後，你會看到臺灣廟宇中最罕見的景象。Walk around the back of the hall to see the rarest sight in Taiwanese temples.
  3. 三百年的守護一代又一代的人，為這五位女子修墓、寫詩、種樹。Generation after generation, people repaired the tomb, wrote poems and planted trees.

認識景點

About the place

王爺與五妃The prince and the five

寧靖王朱術桂：如假包換的明朝宗室親王，隨鄭氏渡海來臺，守著對舊王朝的忠誠。 王府：鄭經為他在府城建了王府；那座王府後來成為大天后宮。 施琅攻下澎湖：1683 年，施琅率清軍水師大敗鄭軍，投降只是幾天內的事。 五位妃子：袁氏、王氏、秀姑、梅姐、荷姐。王爺說她們可以自行打算，她們卻選擇追隨王爺。

Zhu Shugui, Prince of Ningjing: A real Ming prince who followed the Zheng family to Taiwan. The prince's residence: His residence in the city later became the Grand Matsu Temple. Shi Lang takes Penghu: In 1683, Shi Lang's Qing fleet crushed the Zheng navy at Penghu. The five ladies: Yuan, Wang, Xiugu, Meijie and Hejie. The prince told them they were free to go, but they chose to stay with him.

五妃的回答：史書記載：王爺能為國死，我們就能為王爺死。她們在王府中堂離世，王爺親手安葬了她們。 絕命詩：王爺寫下「艱辛避海外，總為數莖髮」，不願剃髮降清，隨後也殉國。依傳統算法享年六十六歲。

Their answer: If the prince could die for his dynasty, they could die for their prince. The farewell poem: He wrote that he had endured exile overseas, all for a few strands of hair, and soon followed them.

廟墓合一A temple and a tomb in one

魁斗山：當年是南門外的荒涼小丘；文人取諧音，雅稱「桂子山」。 1746 年修墓建廟：兩位巡臺御史被故事打動，下令修墓建廟，立「寧靖王從死五妃墓」碑。 墳塚在殿後：墳塚就隆起在正殿正後方；墓碑嵌在神像後方的牆上。 義靈君祠：廟旁小祠，祭祀兩位同樣殉死的侍宦；他們的名字沒有留下來。

Kuidou Shan: Once a lonely hill outside the south gate, poets also called it Guizi Shan. A proper tomb in 1746: Two imperial censors ordered a proper tomb and shrine, with a stele naming the five. The mound behind the altar: The grave mound rises directly behind the altar, so the hall and tomb are one. Shrine of the Righteous Spirits: A small shrine honors two attendants whose names nobody knows.

太監宮女門神：門上畫的不是威武武將，而是太監與宮女，像在王府裡服侍主人。 王爺不葬在這裡：寧靖王與元配羅氏合葬，墓在今天的高雄湖內。

Gentle door gods: The doors show palace attendants and maids, not fierce generals. The prince lies elsewhere: The prince is buried with his wife in Hunei, Kaohsiung.

三百年的守護Three centuries of care

1927 年大整修：經費來自日治時期的婦女組織「愛國婦人會」；三百年後，為五位女性守墓的又是一群女性。 綠地公園：1929 年四周整建成公園，至今仍是府城最安靜的角落之一。 五星同光：文人常把五妃比作五顆星；「五星同光」匾毀於二戰空襲。 國定古蹟：1983 年列為第一級古蹟，今為國定古蹟。

The 1927 restoration: A women's association paid for it, so women once again cared for these women. A green park: Since 1929 the grounds have been a quiet green park. Five bright stars: Poets compared the five ladies to five bright stars. A national monument: It was listed as a first-class monument in 1983.

第一個古蹟保存區：2020 年，周邊成為全臺第一個古蹟保存區，新房子要配合古廟的風貌。 故事的兩半：五妃的選擇是忠貞的；但在那個時代，失去王府的女子也幾乎沒有別的路可走。兩半都說，才對得起她們。

The first conservation area: In 2020, its neighborhood became Taiwan's first heritage conservation area. Both halves of the story: Their choice was loyal, and their world also gave them very few other choices. A good guide tells both halves.
