德化堂Dehua Hall

認識齋堂如何成為封建時代婦女的安身之所，以及德化堂這座「像老宅的佛堂」的故事。

Learn how vegetarian halls sheltered women in a feudal age, and the story of a temple that looks like a house.

府前路上有一座安靜的齋堂，它講的不是帝王將相，而是尋常女子。清代的齋堂可說是婦女庇護所。未婚、婚姻不幸或喪偶的女性可以在這裡安身修行。堂裡的人不剃度、不穿僧袍，但持齋茹素。女性稱菜姑，男性稱菜公。德化堂由龍華派信徒創建於清道光年間。正殿供奉觀音，三張空椅紀念三位祖師。1918 年附近的德善堂併入。後來開路拆掉前落，二戰空襲又炸毀了左右廂房。今天它仍像一戶體面的老宅。四大天王守著大門，門後的牌位記著一代代堂眾。

On Fuqian Road stands a quiet hall that is not about emperors or generals, but about ordinary women. In the Qing dynasty, a vegetarian hall was a kind of women's shelter. Unmarried, unhappily married or widowed women could live and practice here. The residents didn't shave their heads or wear robes, but they kept a vegetarian diet. Women were called cai-gu, and men cai-gong. Dehua Hall was founded in the 1830s by lay Buddhists of the Longhua school. Guanyin sits on the altar, and three empty chairs honor its founders. In 1918, a nearby hall merged into it. Later, a new road took its front courtyard, and air raids destroyed its side wings. Today it still looks like a dignified old house. Behind doors painted with the Four Heavenly Kings, tablets remember generations of members.

認識景點

About the place

尋常女子的庇護所A shelter for ordinary women

齋堂：清代臺灣的齋教信徒在齋堂裡持齋、念經、修行。 婦女庇護所：未婚、婚姻不幸或喪偶的女性可以入堂，幫忙雜務、獲得食宿並修行，免受社會壓力。 收留老人：許多齋堂也收留無家可歸、無後代奉養的老人。 不剃度、不穿僧袍：堂裡的人並非出家：不剃度、不著僧袍，但持齋禮佛。

vegetarian hall: In a vegetarian hall, lay believers kept a meat-free diet and chanted together. A women's refuge: Unmarried, unhappily married or widowed women could help with chores, receive food and lodging, and practice in peace. Care for the elderly: Many halls also took in homeless and elderly people with no family. Not monks or nuns: They didn't shave their heads or wear robes, but they kept a vegetarian diet.

菜姑與菜公：女性稱「菜姑」，男性稱「菜公」。

Vegetable aunts and elders: The women were called cai-gu, and the men were called cai-gong.

德化堂的故事The story of Dehua Hall

在家佛教：龍華派信徒受三皈五戒、持齋，在家修行，也可以結婚。 兩尊觀音：1918 年德善堂因開路拆除併入德化堂，正殿因此供奉兩尊觀音。 被道路切掉的前落：1923 年開闢今天的府前路，拆掉了最前面的一落。 戰火與重建：二戰空襲炸毀左右廂房，戰後陸續整修，2000 年完成大修。

lay Buddhist: Longhua believers took Buddhist vows and kept a vegetarian diet, but lived as lay people. Two Guanyin statues: In 1918, nearby Deshan Hall merged into Dehua Hall, so the altar holds two Guanyin statues. A road through the front: In 1923, road building for today's Fuqian Road took away the front courtyard. War and rebuilding: Air raids in World War II destroyed the side wings. A major restoration was finished in 2000.

珍貴的經版：堂內保存清代到日治時期的木刻經版，見證臺灣早期印刷史。

Precious woodblocks: The hall keeps old woodblocks for printing scriptures, a record of early printing in Taiwan.

看這裡！Look closer

一張書香人家的臉：平直的屋脊、內斂的色彩。這份低調正是重點：安靜的房子，住著安靜的人生。 四大天王：大門畫著四大天王，手持寶劍、琵琶、雨傘等，象徵風調雨順。 三公椅：羅祖像前的三張空椅，代表龍華派三位祖師。 歷代牌位：堂內供奉歷代堂主與齋友的牌位，記錄這座齋堂的社會史。

A scholar's face: Straight ridgelines and restrained colors: a quiet house for quiet lives. The Four Heavenly Kings: on the doors wish for good wind and rain. Three empty chairs: honor the three patriarchs of the Longhua school. Memorial tablets: Tablets honor past hall masters and members, the social history of the hall.

潘麗水的水墨：府城彩繪名師潘麗水在堂內留下水墨作品。 獅咬花籃：正殿山牆上的獅子咬著花籃，祈求四季平安，也有避邪之意。

Ink paintings by Pan Li-shui: The master painter Pan Li-shui left ink paintings inside the hall. Lion with a flower basket: A lion holding a flower basket on the gable wishes for peace and wards off evil.
