四草綠色隧道Sicao Green Tunnel

歡迎來到四草綠色隧道，台灣的小亞馬遜，位在台江國家公園。在大眾廟旁，遊客穿上救生衣、戴上斗笠，搭竹筏駛進紅樹林。這裡長著四種紅樹林，拱起的支持根和呼吸根，搭出了綠色隧道。底下的泥灘，到處是揮著單邊大螯的招潮蟹，和會走路的魚：彈塗魚。這條水道是內海時代的舊水路，潟湖淤積後，還為運鹽的船服役了許多年。附近還有 1840 年為守護海岸而建的四草砲台。

Welcome to the Sicao Green Tunnel, Taiwan's little Amazon, in Taijiang National Park. Beside the Dazhong Temple, visitors put on life vests and bamboo hats, and ride a bamboo raft into the mangroves. Four kinds of mangrove grow here. Their arched prop roots and breathing roots build the green tunnel. Below, the mud is full of fiddler crabs waving one giant claw, and mudskippers, the fish that walk. This water lane is an old channel from the inland-sea era. Salt boats used it long after the lagoon silted up. Nearby stands the Sicao Fort, built in 1840 to guard the coast. Let's start exploring!

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history

四草綠色隧道被稱為台灣的小亞馬遜，位在台江國家公園。竹筏駛進兩岸紅樹林合攏而成的綠色隧道，這條水道正是昔日內海的舊水路，附近還有清代的四草砲台。

The Sicao Green Tunnel, called Taiwan's little Amazon, is in Taijiang National Park. Rafts glide through mangroves that meet overhead along an old waterway of the inland sea, near the Qing-era Sicao Fort.

  1. 1840鴉片戰爭期間建四草砲台（鎮海城），今為國定古蹟。Sicao Fort is built during the Opium War; it is now a national monument.
  2. 內海淤積後 After the silting水道仍為運鹽船服役多年。The channel still serves salt boats for many years.
  3. 2009台江國家公園成立。Taijiang National Park is founded.

認識景點

About the place

綠色隧道：兩岸紅樹林在頭頂合攏，形成綠色隧道。 台江國家公園：綠色隧道屬於台江國家公園。 竹筏：以散步的速度前進，航程大約三十分鐘。 救生衣：竹筏上每個人都要穿救生衣。

green tunnel: The mangroves meet overhead and make a green tunnel. Taijiang National Park: The green tunnel is part of Taijiang National Park. bamboo raft: The bamboo raft moves at strolling pace for about thirty minutes. life vest: Everyone on the raft wears a life vest.

斗笠：遊客在竹筏上戴著農夫的斗笠。 紅樹林：四種紅樹林擠滿了兩岸。 支持根：拱起的支持根把紅樹林撐在軟泥上。 呼吸根：從泥裡冒出來，幫樹呼吸空氣。

bamboo hat: Visitors wear farmer's bamboo hats on the raft. mangrove: Four species of mangrove crowd the banks. prop roots: Arched prop roots hold the mangrove up in the soft mud. breathing roots: Breathing roots poke up from the mud to take in air.

招潮蟹：揮舞著單邊的大螯。 彈塗魚：是一種會在泥灘上走路的魚。 小白鷺：全身雪白，黑腳、黃腳趾。 蒼鷺：冬天，蒼鷺會來到濕地。

fiddler crab: A fiddler crab waves one giant claw. mudskipper: The mudskipper is a fish that walks on the mud. little egret: The little egret is white, with black legs and yellow feet. grey heron: Grey herons come to the wetland in winter.

黑面琵鷺：冬天運氣好，還能看到黑面琵鷺。 虱目魚：魚塭裡養著虱目魚，牠的名字一說來自西拉雅語「麻薩末」。 白頭翁：停車場樹梢上唱歌的鳥，是白頭翁。 舊水道：這條水道是運鹽船曾經使用的舊水路。

black-faced spoonbill: With luck, you may see a black-faced spoonbill in winter. milkfish: Milkfish are raised in the ponds. Its Taiwanese name may come from the Siraya word masame. light-vented bulbul: The bird singing by the parking lot is a light-vented bulbul. old channel: This water lane is an old channel that salt boats once used.

磚造殘蹟：岸邊的磚造殘蹟，與荷蘭時代及後世海關廳舍有關。 四草大眾廟：主祀鎮海大元帥，相傳是鄭成功的部將陳酉。 四草砲台：（鎮海城）建於 1840 年鴉片戰爭期間，由姚瑩督建，今為國定古蹟。

brick remnants: Brick remnants by the bank are linked to the Dutch era and later customs posts. Dazhong Temple: The Dazhong Temple honors the Marshal of the Sea, a general under Koxinga. Sicao Fort: The Sicao Fort was built in 1840, during the Opium War, to guard the coast.
