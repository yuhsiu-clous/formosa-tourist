開基天后祖廟（小媽祖廟）Kaiji Mazu Ancestral Temple

台南人叫它「小媽祖廟」。別被騙了！它比「大媽祖廟」還老四十多年。神壇上那尊小小的神像更老：「船仔媽」1640 年在湄洲雕成。它隨鄭成功的艦隊渡海來台，是艦隊的鎮風之神。據廟方沿革，1662 年鄭成功在這裡建媽祖廟，連磚頭都是從廈門用船運來的。所以它冠上了府城最驕傲的兩個字：「開基」，最早開創的那一間。1777 年，蔣元樞把它擴建為三進，並為後殿獻上一尊傾聽觀音。門口的對聯不頌神蹟，而是在抗議！上下聯第一個字合起來正是「天后」。找找三爪龍柱、罕見的魚龍雀替，還有扛著屋角的憨番。

Tainan calls this the Little Mazu Temple. Do not be fooled! It is more than forty years older than the Grand Mazu Temple. The little statue on its altar is older still. The Boat Mazu was carved on Meizhou in 1640. It sailed to Taiwan with Koxinga's fleet as its wind-calming guardian. According to the temple's records, Koxinga built a Mazu temple here in 1662. Even the bricks came from Xiamen by ship. So it took the proudest word in Tainan: kaiji, the founding one. In 1777, Chiang Yuan-shu enlarged it into three halls and gave a Listening Guanyin for the rear hall. At the front door, the couplet does not praise miracles. It protests! Its first two characters spell Tianhou, Empress of Heaven. Look for the three-clawed dragon columns, the rare fish-dragon brackets, and the foreigners shouldering the roof corners.

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history

開基天后祖廟雖被叫作「小媽祖廟」，卻比大天后宮更早。廟名的「開基」是府城最驕傲的字眼，代表最早開創的那一間；神壇上的「船仔媽」據說是 1640 年雕成，隨鄭成功船隊來台。

Locals call it the little Mazu temple, but it is older than the Grand Matsu Temple. 'Kaiji' means the first one founded. The small statue on its altar is said to have been carved in 1640 and to have sailed with Koxinga's fleet.

  1. 1640（相傳 legend）船仔媽在湄洲雕成，隨鄭成功船隊渡海。The boat Mazu statue is said to be carved in Meizhou and to sail with Koxinga's fleet.
  2. 1662（廟方沿革）據廟方沿革，鄭成功在此建媽祖廟，磚頭從廈門運來。According to the temple, Koxinga builds a Mazu temple here with bricks shipped from Xiamen.
  3. 1777蔣元樞擴建為三進，並獻上一尊傾聽觀音。Official Jiang Yuanshu enlarges it into three halls and gives a listening Guanyin.
  4. 今天 Today國定古蹟。A national monument.

認識景點

About the place

祖廟：一位神明最早的廟，後來的廟都尊它為祖。 神像：雕刻的神明像。船仔媽雕於 1640 年。 對聯：掛在門兩側、成對的句子。這副對聯是在抗議。 龍柱：雕著龍的石柱。這裡的龍是三爪龍。

ancestral temple: The first temple of a god, which later temples look up to. statue: A carved figure of a god. The Boat Mazu statue was carved in 1640. couplet: Two matching lines hung beside a door. This one protests instead of praising. dragon column: A stone column carved with a dragon. Here the dragons have three claws.

開基：「開基」就是最早開創的那一間，府城最驕傲的字眼。 艦隊：一群船。船仔媽隨鄭成功的艦隊來台。 磚頭：蓋廟的磚頭是第二批艦隊從廈門運來的。 天后：媽祖的封號。廟名因此改為開基天后宮。

the founding one: Kaiji means the founding one, the proudest word in Tainan. fleet: A group of ships. The Boat Mazu sailed with Koxinga's fleet. bricks: The temple's bricks came by ship from Xiamen with the second fleet. Empress of Heaven: A title given to Mazu. Then the temple became Kaiji Tianhou Temple.

傾聽觀音：斜坐、低頭，像在俯身傾聽的觀音像。 匾額：寫著大字的木匾。林朝英 1808 年寫了「慈慧」匾。 門神：畫在門上的守護將軍：秦叔寶與尉遲恭。 魚龍雀替：樑柱交接處、造型特殊的雀替。

Listening Guanyin: A Guanyin statue that leans and bows her head, as if listening. plaque: A board with big characters. Lin Chao-ying wrote the Ci Hui plaque in 1808. door gods: Guardian generals painted on the doors: Qin Shubao and Yuchi Gong. fish-dragon bracket: A rare carved bracket where the beam meets the column.

扛：用肩膀扛。屋角下有「憨番」扛著廟角。 國定古蹟：國家指定保護的古蹟。這間廟如今就是。

shoulder: To carry on your shoulders. Little foreigners shoulder the roof corners. national monument: A historic site protected by the nation. This temple is one today.
