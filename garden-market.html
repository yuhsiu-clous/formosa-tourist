花園夜市The Garden Night Market

整趟旅程的結尾只有一種正確方式：吃！歡迎來到花園夜市！問台南人今天哪個夜市有開，他會唸一句咒語：「大大武花，大武花。」聽起來像咒語，其實是一張行事曆。「大」是大東夜市，「武」是武聖夜市，「花」就是花園夜市。從星期一唸到星期天，就知道每天晚上是誰開門。台南的大型夜市是「游牧式」的：只在一週的特定幾晚，在大片空地上開張。數百個攤位在旗海下亮成一片。先上天橋看全景！從烤魷魚、棺材板、現打芒果汁，到夜市遊戲和手機殼，應有盡有。導覽員的策略：餓著肚子來，先逛完一圈再出手，所有東西都分食，跟著最長的在地人隊伍排。

We end our journey the only proper way: eating. Welcome to the Garden Night Market! Ask a Tainan local which night market is open tonight, and you will hear a magic spell: Da-Da-Wu-Hua, Da-Wu-Hua. It sounds like a spell, but it's a calendar. Da is the Dadong market, Wu is Wusheng, and Hua is Huayuan, the Garden Night Market. Read it from Monday to Sunday, and you know who is open each night. Tainan's great night markets are nomadic. Each opens only on certain nights of the week, on huge open lots. Hundreds of stalls glow under a sea of banner flags. Climb the pedestrian overpass for the full panorama. You can find grilled squid, coffin bread and fresh mango juice, and even carnival games and phone cases. Guide's strategy: arrive hungry, walk one full lap before buying anything, share everything, and follow the longest local queue.

為什麼值得來

Why it matters & history

花園夜市是台南最有名的大型夜市之一。台南的大夜市輪流開張，數百個攤位在旗海下亮成一片，從烤魷魚、棺材板到現打芒果汁都吃得到，是體驗台南夜生活的好地方。

The Garden Night Market is one of Tainan's best-known night markets. The big markets here take turns opening on different nights, and hundreds of stalls light up under a sea of flags.

  1. 每週 Every week在地口訣「大大武花，大武花」：花園夜市是週四和週日開。Locals use a rhyme to remember the schedule: the Garden Night Market opens on Thursdays and Sundays.
  2. 導覽員小提醒 Guide's tip餓著肚子來、先逛一圈、所有東西分著吃、跟著最長的在地人隊伍排。Come hungry, walk one full loop first, share everything, and join the longest local line.

認識景點

About the place

攤位／攤販：攤位是小小的販賣台；攤販是在那裡賣東西的人。 記憶口訣：幫助記憶的短句。「大大武花，大武花」就是一週的夜市。 游牧式的：四處移動的。每個大夜市只在特定幾晚開張。 烤魷魚：在火上烤、串在竹籤上吃的魷魚。

stall / vendor: A stall is a small shop table. A vendor is the person who sells there. mnemonic: A short saying that helps you remember. Da-Da-Wu-Hua, Da-Wu-Hua is a week of night markets. nomadic: Moving from place to place. Each big night market opens only on certain nights. grilled squid: Squid cooked over a fire and eaten on a stick.

攤旗、旗海：數百面直立的攤旗在夜空下亮成一片。 天橋：讓行人過馬路的橋；爬上去可以看夜市全景。 棺材板：炸吐司盒，裡面盛著濃郁的燴料。 現打芒果汁：當場現打的芒果汁。

banner flags: Hundreds of tall stall flags glowing against the night sky. pedestrian overpass: A bridge for people to walk over a road. Climb it to see the whole market. coffin bread: A fried toast box filled with creamy stew. fresh mango juice: Mango juice blended right in front of you.

排隊（人龍）：排隊的人龍。跟著最長的在地人隊伍排！ 分享、分食：所有東西都分著吃，才能嚐到更多。

queue: A line of people waiting. Follow the longest local queue! share: Share everything, so you can taste more.
