安平金小姐像The Statue of Miss Jin

每座港都都有自己的愛情故事，安平最有名的一段，化成了一首歌。1951 年，作詞家陳達儒與作曲家許石寫下台語歌謠《安平追想曲》。故事背景是十九世紀末，《天津條約》讓安平開港通商之後。一位安平商人的女兒，愛上了一位荷蘭船醫。他們生下一個金髮女孩，人稱「金小姐」。金小姐長大後，命運重演：她也愛上一位途經安平港的外國人。這個男人，就像她的父親一樣，一去不回。2011 年，奇美文化基金會捐贈了這座青銅雕像，就立在東興洋行前。雕塑家陳正雄以細膩的刀法刻畫這對母女。兩人都望向港口的出海口──船離開的方向，永遠地等待著。

Every port city has its love stories. Anping's most famous one became a song. In 1951, lyricist Chen Da-ru and composer Xu Shi wrote a Taiwanese ballad, Anping Reminiscence. The story is set in the late nineteenth century, after the Treaty of Tianjin opened Anping to foreign trade. The daughter of an Anping merchant fell in love with a Dutch ship's doctor. They had a golden-haired girl. People called her Miss Jin, Miss Gold. When Miss Jin grew up, fate repeated itself. She fell in love with a foreigner passing through the port. Like her father before him, he sailed away and never came back. In 2011, the Chi Mei Cultural Foundation donated this bronze statue. It stands in front of the Julius Mannich and Company Merchant House. The sculptor, Chen Zheng-xiong, shows the mother and daughter with delicate detail. Both of them gaze toward the harbor mouth, the way the ship left. They are waiting forever.

Why it matters & history

安平金小姐像立在東興洋行前，紀念台語歌謠《安平追想曲》。歌曲描寫安平開港後，商人之女與荷蘭船醫的女兒「金小姐」，兩代人望著港口等待的故事。

The Statue of Miss Jin stands in front of the Julius Mannich merchant house. It honors the Taiwanese song 'Memories of Anping', about a merchant's daughter and her fair-haired daughter who both wait at the harbor for men who never return.

About the place

青銅雕像：用青銅鑄成的人像；這座是一對母女。 歌謠、敘事歌：說故事的歌。《安平追想曲》是一首台語歌謠。 追想、回憶：帶著深刻感情回想過去。 作詞家：寫歌詞的人；陳達儒寫了歌詞。

bronze statue: A figure made of bronze, a brown metal. This one shows a mother and her daughter. ballad: A song that tells a story. Anping Reminiscence is a Taiwanese ballad. reminiscence: Remembering the past with deep feeling. lyricist: A person who writes the words of a song. Chen Da-ru wrote the lyrics.

作曲家：寫曲子的人；許石譜了曲。 雕塑家：創作雕像的藝術家；這座出自陳正雄之手。 天津條約：這個條約讓安平開港，與外國通商。 商人：做買賣的人；母親是商人的女兒。

金小姐像 The Statue of Miss Jin

這座金小姐和母親的青銅雕像，2011 年在東興洋行前揭幕，靈感來自 1951 年的歌謠《安平追想曲》。相傳，金小姐的父親是一位荷蘭船醫。

This bronze statue of Miss Jin and her mother was unveiled in 2011, in front of the Julius Mannich house. It comes from the 1951 song Anping Reminiscence. It is said that Miss Jin's father was a Dutch ship's doctor.
